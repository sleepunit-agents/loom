# Philosophy

Eurorack is not a DAW. It is a different kind of instrument — one that resists
the mouse-and-menus workflow entirely. The noodle-first principle: explore first,
record later.

## The Lens

Every module is a lens on sound. The Strega has a built-in LPG and spring reverb.
You can't play it wrong, only boring.

Hermod+ is a trade candidate because it introduces DAW-like precision that works
against the philosophy.

## Why Eurorack

The zero-to-sound test: how quickly can you make something interesting? A system
that passes in under 30 seconds is a good system.
