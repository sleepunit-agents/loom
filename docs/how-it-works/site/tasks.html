Tasks

The household's work queue. A task is one piece of work with a plain title, a goal and a reason.

  • Shows what is ready to pick up, most urgent first.
  • Lets a person or an agent claim work, comment on it, close it, and hand it back or reopen it.
  • Groups work under a parent and holds back what is blocked.
  • Carries a question to a person when the work needs a decision.

How it works

Ready, blocked, comments and questions

Four behaviours cover what the app does.

Ready

  • It is open
  • It is not an epic
  • No open task blocks it
READY
openclaimin progresscloseclosed

A task is ready when it is open, is not an epic and nothing blocks it. Claiming it moves it to in progress, and unclaiming puts it back. Closing it takes a reason, and the reason cancelled marks it cancelled. A closed task can be reopened.

Parents and blockers

A task sits under one parent at most. An epic groups other tasks and is never ready itself. A blocked task waits until the task blocking it closes.

Also in Jonathan's Inbox

A task can carry one question, with what happens on yes and on no. The person answers yes or no, or in words. The question also appears in that person's Inbox.

Who can see this

projectowner
  • aEvery task has an owner. A member, an agent or the household. There is no task without one.
  • bWho sees it. Its project's people when it has a project. Otherwise its owner, and for an agent's task, that agent's owners. Household owners always.
  • cChecked at the door. Loom applies this to callers from outside: a person, an agent, a machine, someone's own program. Loom's own parts read tasks without asking.
