People and projects

Who is in the household, which agents they own, and which projects they share. Everything else in loom points back at these three: members, agents and projects.

  • Lists the members: people who can sign in.
  • Lists the agents, and who owns each.
  • Puts people and agents on projects so they can work together.
  • Supplies the labels every other part uses to decide who sees what.

How it works

Members, agents, projects and household settings

Members and owners

  • ownerhousehold authority
  • full ownerowns every agent, present and future
  • memberaccess through projects and owned agents

A member is a person who can sign in. A household owner sees everything, including another member's private thread. Everyone else reaches things through their projects and the agents they own.

Agents

Artowned by jonathan
Markowned by jonathan, sam

An agent is one of the household's assistants. A member owns any number of agents, and an agent can have more than one owner. Each agent has a short description of itself, badge colours and a drawn face.

Projects

ProjectPondmembers jonathan, sam · agent art · repository github:owner/repo

A project is the unit of sharing. Put a person and an agent on the same project and they can work together on it. A project also lists its code repositories.

Household and preferences

  • householddefault agent, time zone, how long words are kept, backups
  • each memberlook, desk background, sound, motion, contrast

One row holds the household-wide settings. Each person has their own preferences for how loom looks and behaves.

Who can see this

projectagentmember, when private
  • aThe rule. A household owner sees everything, including what is private to another member. Anyone else sees what is in their projects, plus what belongs to agents they own.
  • bPrivate. Private to a member means that member plus household owners.
  • cTasks. A task with a project is seen by the project's people. A task without one is seen by its owner, and for an agent's task, by that agent's owners.
  • dChecked at the door. Loom applies this to callers from outside: a person, an agent, a machine, someone's own program. Loom's own parts read without asking.
