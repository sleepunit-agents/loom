Discord and Slack

How agents talk to people in other apps. A connection is one bot signed in to one Discord server or Slack workspace, speaking for one agent.

  • Connects a bot to a server or workspace for one agent.
  • Decides which agent answers in a channel when nobody is addressed.
  • Links an outside user to the household member they are.
  • Records whether each reply arrived, and says so when it cannot tell.

How it works

Messages in, replies out, channel rules and connections

In

pendingmatcheddelivered

A message from outside is kept as an incoming message, matched to a person and a channel rule, and passed into the right conversation. It can be held or dropped, with a reason.

Out

pendingsendingsent

The agent's reply goes out as a send. Loom records whether it arrived. Uncertain means loom could not confirm it, and a person reviews it. A held send waits for a person to release or dismiss it.

Who answers

  • answersthe agent that replies to messages addressed to nobody
  • bots may talkwhether bots may talk to each other here
  • only / ignorechannels a connection listens to or skips

A channel rule names the agent that answers in a channel. A connection can listen only to some channels, or ignore some.

Connections and files

  • stateconnecting, connected, backoff, disconnected
  • filesSlack attachments, kept 14 days

A connection is one bot signed in to one Discord server or Slack workspace, speaking for one agent. Attachments fetched from Slack are kept for 14 days.

Who can see this

  • aHousehold things. Connections, channel rules and person links belong to the household. Household owners manage them and see them all.
  • bAn agent's owners. An agent's owners see their agent's connections.
  • cMessages. An incoming message follows the labels of the conversation it goes into. A household owner sees everything, including what is private to another member.
  • dChecked at the door. Loom applies this to callers from outside: a person, an agent, a machine, someone's own program. Loom's own parts read without asking.
