The desk

The desk is loom on a desktop screen. App tiles side by side, the agents on a rail, a ticker along the top and a dock along the bottom.

  • Places tiles by one rule, so the desk is full and nothing overlaps.
  • Keeps every installed app within reach in the launcher and the dock.
  • Shows each agent's face, its state and what it is doing.
  • Wears one of three looks: Neon Noir, Low Light or Daylight.

How it works

Tiling, the launcher, faces and looks

Tiling

  • under 1280 px2 tiles
  • from 12803 tiles
  • from 16004 tiles
  • from 25606 tiles

The first tile takes the big slot. The others share the space beside it on a wide screen, or below it on a tall one. When one more will not fit, the least recently used tile goes to the dock. A tile can be made the big one, minimized to the dock, or closed.

Launcher and dock

AppsType to find an appWork · People & agents · Library · System · Look & feel · Pieces

The launcher lists every app in groups and finds one as you type. Enter opens the first match. Every installed app also stays within reach in the dock.

Faces

Artworking
Markquiet

Each agent has a drawn face: one of four shapes (pebble, trefoil, clover, drop) with dot or oval eyes. Its expression is its state: quiet, thinking, working, waiting on you, done, needs a look, talking.

Looks

  • Neon Noirloud and lit
  • Low Lightthe same, calmer
  • Daylightlight

A look is a set of colours and type over one structure. A person also picks the desk background (depth, contours or wash), reduced motion, higher contrast, and one window at a time.

Who can see this

  • aWhat you may see. The desk shows each person only what they may see. Every tile gets its rows through the same visibility check.
  • bYour own look. The look, the desk background and the switches belong to each member.
  • cThe phone. The phone does what the desk does, built after the desk is right.
