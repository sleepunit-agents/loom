Inbox

One inbox for everything sent to anyone, person or agent. The Inbox app is a person's side of it: the items addressed to you that are waiting.

  • Lists what needs you, with what happens on yes and on no.
  • Takes a yes, a no, or an answer in your own words.
  • Sends you to Approvals or the task when the answer belongs there.
  • Marks what you have read and keeps what was answered.

How it works

Recipients, questions, settling and read marks

Two directions, one table

  • to an agentits body takes the item
  • to a personit waits in their Inbox app

Every item has a recipient. An agent's body takes the items addressed to it, in order. A person's Inbox app is the items addressed to them that are waiting.

A question

Question · yes or noSpend up to $180 on the pump?If yes, Art orders it today. If no, Art looks for a cheaper one.

An item that is a question says how it is answered, with yes or no or in words, and what happens on each answer. The answer, who gave it and when are recorded on the item. Loom also adds a new item for the agent in the same conversation, with answer as its source and the question as its source id.

Settled without an answer

waitingits task closesresolved

A question can name an approval or a task whose outcome settles it. When that finishes, the item resolves and records why.

Read marks and references

  1. inbox_readsjonathan opened it, 9:21 AM
  2. inbox_refst-2190
  3. inbox_refsgithub:owner/repo#12

Opening an item marks it read for that member. An item lists the tasks, approvals, pull requests or conversations it is about.

Who can see this

recipientprojectagentmember, when private
  • aThe recipient. An item is for one person or one agent. A person's Inbox shows the waiting items addressed to them.
  • bWho else sees it. An item also carries the usual labels: a project's people, an agent's owners, or one member alone. A household owner sees everything, including what is private to another member. Private to a member means that member plus household owners.
  • cChecked at the door. Loom applies this to callers from outside: a person, an agent, a machine, someone's own program. Loom's own parts read without asking.
