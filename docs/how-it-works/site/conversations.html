Conversations

A conversation is one agent talking in one place. Loom keeps one record for each, whatever the place, so a chat in loom, a Discord channel, a scheduled run and a handoff all look the same to every other part.

  • One tableEvery conversation is one row.
  • One per placeAn agent has one conversation per thread, channel or lane.
  • One job at a timeAt most one job works in a conversation.
Where

Five places a conversation happens

The word in colour is what loom writes in the conversation's venue column. Within a venue, the place is a thread, a channel, a lane name or, for a handoff, the parent job.

loomA thread in loom

A private chat in Comms between one person and one agent.

discordDiscord

A channel or a direct message. A bot signed in to the server speaks for the agent.

slackSlack

The same, in a Slack workspace.

wakeA trigger

A trigger from Tempo drops an item in the agent's inbox, such as a lane that runs every night.

handoffA subagent

A piece of work one agent hands to another. Its place is the parent job.

In, and back out

One inbox for everyone

Everything sent to anyone, person or agent, lands in one inbox that knows where each item came from. Think of a mail program: each source is an account, and there is still one combined inbox to read and search.

Turns

Each thing said or done is a turn: a message, a reply, an action, a wake, a note or a refusal. Loom records who it came from, when, and how it went. A turn holds no words.

DigestPicked a pump that fits the old hose. Ready to order.
  • decisionThe middle model, $164
  • taskt-2190 Order the pump
  • askSpend up to $180?

A digest

When an agent finishes a stretch of work it writes a digest: a summary, what was decided, the tasks and projects it touched, and anything it needs from its owner.

Inbox item · waitingSpend up to $180 on the pump Art picked? If yes, Art orders it today. If no, Art looks for a cheaper one.YesNo

A question

That need becomes a question in the inbox, addressed to a person, with what happens on each answer. The answer, who gave it and when are recorded on the same item, and loom adds a new item for the agent, with answer as its source, so the agent learns of it.

How it connects to every other part

Who can see this

agentprojectmember, when private
  • aThree labels. Each conversation carries its agent, its project if it has one, and a member when it is private to one person.
  • bWho sees it. A household owner sees everything, including another member's private thread. Anyone else sees conversations in their projects and conversations of agents they own.
  • cPrivate. A thread in loom is private to its member. Private to a member means that member plus household owners.
  • dChecked at the door. Loom applies this to callers from outside: a person, an agent, a machine, someone's own program. Loom's own parts read without asking.
  • eWords. Message words are kept for the household's keep time, 14 days at most, then removed. They are encrypted where they are stored.
